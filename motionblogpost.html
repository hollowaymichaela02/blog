The crowd at Canada's Wonderland: Halloween Haunt, in Vaughan, ON. Taken November 1st, 2026. Photo by Michaela Holloway.

Crowded, foggy, and very spooky... Canada's Wonderland is constantly moving-- whether it's the rollercoasters, thrill rides, or overwhelming crowds. We got lost and walked through the "Kingdom of Carnage" so many times that a scare actor broke character to ask us where the hell we're going.

Canada's Wonderland: Halloween Haunt performance titled "Inferno" in Vaughan, ON. Taken November 1st, 2026. Video by Michaela Holloway.

There was also a really underwhelming fire performance. The actors were pretending that they were outside the literal gates of hell to geometry dash sounding dubstep.
